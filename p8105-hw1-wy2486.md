As shown above, calculating the mean of the character vector and factor
vector produces *NA* (does not work), while it works for the random
sample and logical vector. The random sample is numeric, so its mean can
be calculated directly. For the logical vector, R treats *TRUE as 1* and
*FALSE as 0*, so the mean can also be calculated. The character and
factor vectors are not numeric, so their means cannot be calculated
directly.
